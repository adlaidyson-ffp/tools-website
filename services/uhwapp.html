Upper Haugh Weather

A detailed local weather platform with forecasts, and air quality data, Met Office Alerts, and more!

About this service

Welcome to Upper Haugh Weather—your dedicated source for hyper-local meteorological data across Upper Haugh, Rawmarsh, and the surrounding Rotherham areas. Our mission is to provide residents with the most accurate, up-to-date weather information through a seamless, ad-free experience.

From real-time temperature tracking to specialized atmospheric data, Upper Haugh Weather ensures you’re prepared for whatever the South Yorkshire sky has in store.

Core Features:

  • Precision Tracking: Real-time temperature and conditions in Celsius.
  • Short-Term Planning: Detailed 25-hour hourly forecasts.
  • Long-Range Outlook: Comprehensive 8-day weather projections.
  • At-a-Glance Updates: Dedicated Android Home Screen widget.
  • Deep-Dive Data: Visibility, Pressure, UV Index, and Air Quality (AQI) monitoring.
  • Celestial Tracking: Sun and Moon cycles at your fingertips.
  • Personalized View: Fully optimized Light and Dark modes.

Who it’s for

Any resident of Upper Haugh, or surrounding areas in Rawmarsh, Rotherham.
