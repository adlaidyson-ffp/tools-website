QR Code Generator

This is a simple tool to help you create QR codes quickly.
You can make QR codes for website links or even for your WiFi network, which is handy if you want to share your WiFi password without typing it out.

Once you’ve made your QR code, you can save it as a PNG image and use it anywhere.

The best part? Everything happens right here in your browser, so your info stays private and nothing gets sent anywhere.
